Foreword Before you is a message of the New Age, transmitted through the Ray of Knowledge from June to October 2012. Received by a method of "remote telepathy and radiesthesia", this text reads like a manifesto for an age to come, in which humanity, freed from the shackles of ignorance, becomes the creator of its own reality and the guardian of Earth.
22.06.2012 PART 1 Receive the new tidings. Darkness has been destroyed. The enemy is no more. The forces of light are stronger than ever. We bring new tidings. A change in Life awaits you. A new Life will change you as well. A new Life is already waiting.
23.06.2012 The new conditions will affect everyone. They cannot be avoided. They will touch every corner of Life. Life will not remain unmoved. New vibrations will stir a response in Life. That response will not be pleasant for everyone. But there is no choice. Time will not wait. Change cannot be resisted.
25.06.2012 The new conditions will touch the inner life. A person's relationship with the inner "Self" will change. There is no longer any reason to shut the inner "Self" off from consciousness. The subconscious must have a weighty say in the workings of consciousness. Its word must be heard. The subconscious must connect a person to their higher tasks. These higher tasks must be embraced. Goals must be set. These goals must be worthy of the Human Being. The path to the goal must lead through Knowledge. Only in Knowledge does a person find freedom. Freedom will lead to the goal. The goal will be attained.
26.06.2012 Freedom is the essential condition for reaching the goal. A person must free themselves from all the fetters that bind the "old self" within. Knowledge must break those fetters. Only Knowledge can purify consciousness and set it free. Freedom bestows a new way of thinking. That new way of thinking will lead to the goal. Why do we speak so often of the goal? Because without a goal there is no human being. Without higher goals there is no higher Human Being. To become a higher Human Being is the task before each person. Only Knowledge can help them do so. Only Knowledge will lead them to the goal.
27.06.2012 The goal will be made manifest to consciousness. The subconscious will see to it. The subconscious will reveal a person's higher purpose and define the tasks needed to fulfill it. At first, the tasks will seem difficult, impossible, but in time solving them will bring only joy. Solving these tasks will belong to the realm of higher creativity. Only higher creativity will bring a person happiness. The following tasks belong to the realm of higher creativity: - The task of developing sensitivity of the soul. - The task of developing self-sacrifice. - The task of developing compassion. - The task of developing fearlessness. These are the first tasks; others will follow. A person's ascent up the ladder of the spirit depends on success in these tasks.
28.06.2012 The ladder of the spirit's ascent is very long. Climbing it will take great effort. Every step will be won in battle. But nothing is more triumphant than victory over oneself. In these victories the spirit is tempered. Victories of the spirit will lead from step to step.
29.06.2012 Signs will help on this difficult path. Signs will point the way. The work of Signs has always helped reveal the right path. Sensitivity is needed to recognize those traces.
12.07.2012 A person needs signs. Following them like tracks, a person can reach the truth. Truth is surrounded by signs. The work of signs will live as long as people need them. This will shall bring it to life.

They cannot lift a person toward the light. These differences must be seen. A sensitive heart will not be mistaken. The work of intentions will lift a person.
